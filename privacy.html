Newlight Accounting Integration Privacy Notice

Effective on publication.

Robinson Foods Inc., doing business as Newlight Breadworks, operates Newlight Accounting Integration for internal accounting. This notice covers the integration and the records it handles.

Information we use

The integration supports attended FreshBooks reporting, invoice reconciliation, and QuickBooks sandbox validation. QuickBooks production synchronization is inactive. Depending on the authorized task, the integration may process business and account identifiers, customer and billing contact details, and invoice information such as invoice numbers, dates, line items, prices, taxes, amounts, balances, and status. It also handles connection information and operational records used to diagnose errors and reconcile activity. The integration’s accounting scope excludes payroll, card processing, and online banking credentials.

Use and access

We use this information for internal accounting, invoice checks, reporting, connection administration, and troubleshooting. Authorized Robinson Foods personnel may access information needed for their work. FreshBooks and Intuit provide the connected services, and Google Cloud provides application hosting and storage. The integration’s automated workflow does not call generative AI services. We limit the use of integration data to these accounting and operational purposes and disclosures required by law.

Storage and safeguards

We use restricted cloud storage for connection records and encrypt stored OAuth tokens. We retain active connection records while needed for authorized access. Accounting records, report exports, reconciliation records, and diagnostic or recovery records may remain as needed for accounting, incident review, recovery, or applicable retention obligations. Backups and retained storage versions may persist after a connection is disabled or a record is removed from active use.

Requests and disconnection

Contact stephen@newlightbread.com to request access, correction, removal, or disconnection, or to report a privacy or security concern. We may verify your identity and authority before acting. Disconnection ends the integration’s access once the provider connection is revoked. Accounting records in the source and destination systems, retained reports, and copies required for accounting or legal purposes may remain.
