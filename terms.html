Newlight Accounting Integration Internal Terms of Use

Effective on publication.

Robinson Foods Inc., doing business as Newlight Breadworks, makes Newlight Accounting Integration available to its authorized personnel for internal accounting in its own FreshBooks and QuickBooks accounts. Permission to use the integration is limited to assigned company duties. FreshBooks and QuickBooks remain subject to their respective provider terms.

Only authorized operators may request connection or disconnection. Any production QuickBooks connection requires approval by an authorized company administrator. Keep credentials and connection details in company-approved secure systems. Use approved reporting channels and restrict access to account records to personnel authorized for the relevant work.

The integration currently supports attended read-only reporting and sandbox validation. Robinson Foods’ existing bookkeeping process remains responsible for invoice entry, payment matching, reconciliation decisions, and exception review. Production invoice entry or automatic processing requires separate approval.

To request support, report a possible incident, or ask to disconnect, contact stephen@newlightbread.com. Disconnection changes the integration’s access. Accounting records and retained copies remain subject to the applicable accounting and retention requirements.
