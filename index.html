Newlight Accounting Integration

Newlight Accounting Integration is a private tool for authorized Robinson Foods Inc. personnel. It supports FreshBooks reporting, invoice reconciliation, and QuickBooks sandbox validation. For assistance, contact stephen@newlightbread.com.

Connect or reconnect

An authorized Robinson Foods operator should contact stephen@newlightbread.com to request a connection or reconnection. The company administrator completes any required provider authorization through the approved process.

Disconnect

Contact stephen@newlightbread.com with the provider and company name to request review of an existing connection or confirm that access has ended. Accounting records remain with the provider. Keep credentials and tokens out of email.
